Rules:
- Count a match when an LLM finding identifies the same underlying accessibility problem in the same element or relevant section, even if its wording, certainty, examples, or recommended fix differ from the human audit.
- Treat examples in a Human finding as illustrations unless they clearly describe separate defects. A different remediation recommendation does not invalidate detection.
- Conditional or tentative LLM findings can count when they identify the reported defect and the Human finding supplies supporting context.
- Several LLM findings may cover one Human finding together.
- Reject matches that merely share a WCAG criterion, concern another element, or leave a distinct part of the Human finding undetected. Record such partial evidence in review_notes instead.
- Each LLM finding ID may be used for at most one Human finding on this page.
- Use only LLM finding IDs listed below.
- A Human finding classified "unavailable_evidence" or "ambiguous" cannot be matched: give it no LLM finding IDs.
- The classification and programmatic matches are fixed decisions made earlier. Do not try to change them; they are shown for context only.

Answer for every Human finding listed, exactly once, using its human_finding_id:

- review_notes: anything else worth recording, such as partial matches. Notes don't earn credit. May be empty.
